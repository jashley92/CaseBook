# 0013. Reports are Word documents only

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Recorded:** 2026-10-02

## Context

Organizations have a house report template, and reports get edited before they go out. CaseBook used to
produce PDFs as well, but a PDF can't follow an organization's Word template without Word or LibreOffice on
the server.

## Decision

Reports are generated as `.docx` with DocumentFormat.OpenXml, in one of two ways:

- the built-in layout;
- an administrator-uploaded Word template, filled in through its `{{…}}` fields.

When someone needs a PDF, the user saves the final report as PDF in Word. PDFs generated by older releases
remain stored and downloadable.

## Consequences

- No office suite and no PDF engine on the server.
- Uploaded templates are checked. Files with macros, embedded objects or externally loaded content are
  refused.
- Every report's SHA-256 is recorded, and the stored file can be re-verified against it.
